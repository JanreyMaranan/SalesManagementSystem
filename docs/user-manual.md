\# Hope Inc. SMS — User Manual Draft



\## Overview

The Hope Inc. Sales Management System (SMS) is a web-based application 

for managing sales transactions, customer records, and employee data.



\## User Roles

| Role | Access Level |

|------|-------------|

| USER (Sales Agent) | View sales, view lookups |

| ADMIN (Sales Manager) | Full sales CRUD, view reports |

| SUPERADMIN | Full access including user management |



\## Getting Started

1\. Go to the SMS website

2\. Click "Sign in with Google" or enter your email/password

3\. New accounts require SUPERADMIN activation before first login



\## Modules



\### Sales

\- View all sales transactions

\- ADMIN: Add, Edit, Soft-delete transactions

\- Click a transaction to view Sales Detail



\### Lookups

\- Customers, Employees, Products, Price History

\- Read-only for all users



\### Reports

\- Sales by Customer

\- Top Products by Revenue  

\- Monthly Sales Trend



\### Admin (SUPERADMIN only)

\- View all registered users

\- Activate or Deactivate user accounts



\### Deleted Items (ADMIN/SUPERADMIN only)

\- View soft-deleted sales and details

\- Recover deleted records



\## Logging Out

Click the Sign Out button in the sidebar or navbar.

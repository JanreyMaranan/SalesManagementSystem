\## System Information

\- \*\*App Name:\*\* Hope Inc. Sales Management System

\## Account Setup

1\. Go to the live URL

2\. Click Register — enter your email and password

3\. Wait for SUPERADMIN to activate your account

4\. Login once activated



\## Quick Start Guide



\### Step 1 — Login

\- Enter email and password OR click Sign in with Google

\- Inactive accounts will be redirected with an error message



\### Step 2 — Navigate

\- Use the sidebar to navigate between modules

\- Sidebar links visible based on your role and rights



\### Step 3 — Sales

\- View all transactions on the Sales List page

\- ADMIN: Add new transaction using Add Transaction button

\- Click any transaction row to view its details

\- ADMIN: Edit or soft-delete transactions



\### Step 4 — Reports

\- Go to /reports to view analytics

\- Sales by Customer, Top Products, Monthly Trend



\### Step 5 — Admin (SUPERADMIN only)

\- Go to /admin to manage user accounts

\- Activate new registrations

\- Deactivate inactive users



\## FAQ

\*\*Q: I can't see the Add button\*\*

A: Your account type is USER. Only ADMIN and SUPERADMIN can add transactions.



\*\*Q: I can't access /admin\*\*

A: Only SUPERADMIN accounts have access to the Admin module.



\*\*Q: My login is not working\*\*

A: Your account may be INACTIVE. Contact your SUPERADMIN to activate it.



\*\*Q: I deleted a record by mistake\*\*

A: ADMIN/SUPERADMIN can recover it from the Deleted Items page.



\## Contact

For system issues, contact your system administrator.
